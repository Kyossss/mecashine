Top roller and sensor proposal

The top roller rebuilt the same way as the side brushes: chenille yarn on printed tie discs, a slim hex bar, your motor's shaft fitting kept, with shorter strands so it stays 6 mm under the carriage. The top ToF sensor moves to the other side of the roller, where the shoe stays well clear of it.

Sensor clearance while polishing

ShoeCurrent, 44 mm before the rollerProposed, 44 mm after

A VL53L0X can't measure reliably closer than about 30 mm, and at 7 mm the sensor nearly touches the tongue. Before the roller, the sensor looks at the part of the shoe still coming, up the vamp toward the tongue, which is higher. After the roller it looks at what has just been polished, toward the toe, which is lower. Same height on the carriage, so the home reading to the belt stays 226 mm.

The roller

PartWhat it isPlastic
  • Kept: your motor holder, the D-shaped motor shaft hole, the carriage and its 8 mm pin. The roller fits the same space, y −76 to 67.
  • Brush: 46 mm across when spinning (yarn tips 23 mm from the axis). The carriage's top plate is 29 mm above the axis, so the yarn clears it by 6 mm, room for the chenille's fur. The strands are just cut shorter than on the side brushes; the discs are the same.
  • Yarn: 13 rows of 6 strands (12 tie discs and the idler end's plate): 78 strands cut about 34 mm long, 156 tails, roughly 3 m of chenille.
  • Far end: a 608ZZ bearing in the idler end slides onto the carriage's pin, so the roller no longer turns plastic on plastic.
  • Lowest point: with the carriage at its lowest the yarn stays about 32 mm above the belt.
  • On the shoe: touching for 86 to 91% of the pass, about 1% less than with 52 mm yarn and about the same as the old 50 mm roller.

The new sensor bracket

  • A flange on the downstream end of the carriage's top plate, an arm, and the board 44 mm after the roller axis, facing down at the same height as now.
  • It needs two new M3 holes in the top plate's downstream end, 12 mm either side of the centre. The old bracket and its two screws come off.
  • It stays clear of the lift shaft by 1.5 mm and of the lift brackets, which sit further out.
  • The side and top sensors end up 328 mm apart along the belt, not 240. The firmware uses that distance to time the belt, so the constant changes.

To decide

  • Two holes drilled in the carriage's top plate, or a reprinted carriage with them in.
  • Whether your carriage's 8 mm pin is printed. A 608ZZ needs it close to 8.0 mm; print a short test pin first if it is.
  • Yarn density: 6 strands per disc as on the side brushes.
