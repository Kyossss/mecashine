MecaShine · shoe holder

Shoe carrier: two V-blocks on a plate

A flat plate rides on the belt under the shoe. A fixed V-block cups the heel and a sliding V-block presses against the toe, so the shoe can't slide either way or twist. Both blocks are low and only touch the sole edge, so nothing that needs polish is covered.

What it holds against

  • The top roller pushes the shoe along the belt, one way or the other depending on its spin. The heel V and the toe V stop both directions.
  • The side brushes push sideways and try to twist the shoe. Two Vs, one at each end, centre it and stop the twist.
  • Lifting: nothing in the machine pulls the shoe up, so there's no clamp over it.

Loading a shoe

  1. Loosen the thumb knob and slide the toe block forward.
  2. Put the heel into the heel V. It centres itself.
  3. Slide the toe block back until it touches the toe, then tighten the knob. About 5 seconds.

Why this shape

  • One V fits every size. A narrow heel sits slightly deeper in the V, a wide one slightly further out. The table shows where each size rests.
  • Low parts only. The brushes' yarn and the spray start about 27 mm above the belt, and the IR sensor and side ToF look across at 40 to 42 mm. The heel V stays at 23 mm and the toe block at 17 mm.
  • Nothing far behind the heel. At the start of the polishing pass the heel is close to the entry drum, so the carrier ends 10 mm behind the heel.
  • A wide plate (70 mm) so the sole rests flat and can't rock when the brushes push.

Firmware changes it needs

  • startgap 190 instead of 200, so the back of the carrier stays 6 mm clear of the entry drum on the large shoe.
  • The top sensor sees the toe block (17 mm) just before the toe. Treat anything under about 20 mm as "no shoe" when finding the toe.

Questions

  1. Fixing the plate to the belt: two M3 through one slat (drill 2 holes) is the most solid. Or I can add pockets for the three magnets you already have, so it lifts off. Which do you prefer?
  2. Shoe types: will it only take closed shoes like these, or also sandals, high heels or boots? A tall, narrow heel would need a deeper heel V.
  3. Grip pads: do you want a recess for felt or rubber strips on the V faces, so they don't scuff the sole edge?
