Side spray before the reflector

A nozzle on each side of the belt, just before the reflector, spraying the side of the shoe from higher up and more steeply. An IR sensor on the casing-box side sees the shoe and the pump fires in bursts. The side brushes spread the polish a few seconds later.

What changed in this revision

  • IR sensor on the box side, the left as you look in from the entrance. The shelf and FC-51 are now on that post.
  • Nozzles 25 mm higher. The tip is now 69 mm above the belt (was 50).
  • Tilted 25° down (was 15°).
  • Real box gap: the casing box is drawn 80 mm from the belt, and the post's base plate is 10 mm shorter so it clears it.
  • The posts are the ones you printed, the flat-printing version with the base plate, changed only as above.

Layout

  • Nozzles at x 680, just before the reflector, which starts at x 709 and stays as it is. That's 149 mm before the side ToF sensor.
  • Start position: the firmware backs the shoe up until its toe is startgap = 200 mm before the side sensor, at x 629. That is 51 mm before the nozzles, so every size is sprayed from the toe.
  • Holders: one printed post each side, standing on the base board beside the belt. Its base plate is screwed down by two M3 through the board's existing holes. Nothing touches the belt or its frame.
  • Box gap: I measured your 80 mm from the outside of the belt's side frame, which is 72 mm from the belt centre. That puts the box wall 152 mm from the centre. It also explains the snug fit: the old base plate reached 150. The plate now ends at 140, so it's 12 mm clear. If you measured from the edge of the slats instead (65 mm from the centre), the wall is at 145 and the plate is still 5 mm clear.

Elbow and nozzle mount

  • The elbow bolts on through its own lug, the small hole in its inside corner, with one M3 into a nut in the post. No clip.
  • A seat under the side port sets the 25° downward tilt, so the single screw can't let it turn.
  • The down port points down beside the post, so the hose comes up into it from below.
  • Nozzle tip: 80.5 mm from the centre of the belt (about 30 mm clear of the widest shoe) and 69 mm above the belt. The spray's centre line meets the side of the shoe 50 to 55 mm up (it was about 42), and the spray covers about 20 to 72 mm.
  • Over the toe: the toe is only 50 to 57 mm tall for its first 100 mm, so the top of the spray passes over it there and some lands on the toe cap. Further back, where the shoe is taller, all of it hits the side.

Universal IR mount

  • A small shelf on the box-side post, 26 mm before the nozzles, looking across the belt. It's drawn with your FC-51 / MH-B module on it; switch it off with "Show FC-51".
  • Height: the shelf bolts into one of eight holes in the post, 6 mm apart, which puts the sensor anywhere from 16 to 58 mm above the belt. It's drawn at 40 mm. Both posts have the holes, so the shelf can still go on the far side.
  • Reach: the module's M3 hole sits in a 12 mm slot along the shelf. That puts the LED tips 59 to 71 mm from the belt centre, 9 to 21 mm from the widest shoe. This type of sensor only sees black leather up close, so keep it near the shoe.
  • Fit by the box: the shelf sits between the post and the belt (66 to 112 mm from the centre), so the box doesn't get in the way.
  • Other sensors: two zip-tie slots across the shelf hold any board that doesn't fit the M3 hole.
  • Bursts only on the polishing pass: the first as soon as the sensor sees the shoe, then one 0.4 s burst every few seconds while it still does.

Where the overspray goes

  • Mist that misses the shoe crosses toward the other post. At 25° more of it lands on the belt between the posts than before.
  • The reflector starts 29 mm after the nozzles, so the upstream end of its white insert collects a little. The side ToF sensor is 149 mm away.

Questions

  1. Elbow numbers you're sending: the ones that matter most are the elbow's thickness, the lug hole's size and position, and how far the nozzle sticks out past the blue ring.
  2. Board holes: which existing holes are near x 680 (about 480 mm from the entry edge), their spacing along the belt, how far they are from the belt edge, and the board's thickness.
  3. Height: is a 69 mm nozzle tip high enough? Going higher only makes the posts taller; the tilt stays at 25°.
  4. The 80 mm gap: was it measured from the belt's side frame or from the edge of the slats? Either way the new plate clears the box.
