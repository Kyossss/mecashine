Side spray before the reflector

A nozzle on each side of the belt, just before the reflector, spraying the side of the shoe slightly downward. An IR sensor sees the shoe and the pump fires in bursts. The side brushes spread the polish a few seconds later.

Layout

  • Nozzles at x 680, just before the reflector, which starts at x 709 and stays as it is. That's 149 mm before the side ToF sensor.
  • Start position: the firmware backs the shoe up until its toe is startgap = 200 mm before the side sensor, at x 629. That is 51 mm before the nozzles, so every size is sprayed from the toe.
  • Holders: one printed post each side, standing on the base board beside the belt. Its foot is screwed down with two M3 through the board's existing holes. No crossbar, and nothing touches the belt or its frame.
  • Hose: pump in the box → PU 6×4 along the box side of the board → Y-connector by the near post. One branch goes up that post. The other crosses between the top and return runs of the belt to the far post and up.

Nozzles

  • One elbow per side, on top of its post, 58 mm above the belt, pointing straight across at the shoe and tilted 15° down.
  • Spray band: about 30 to 62 mm up the side of the shoe. Moving the elbow up or down its post moves the band.
  • The nozzle tips are 79 mm from the centre of the belt, which leaves 25 mm to the widest shoe.

IR sensor and bursts

  • IR sensor 15 mm before the far post, 40 mm above the belt, looking across. The heel plate is under 35 mm, so the sensor doesn't see it.
  • Bursts only on the polishing pass: the first as soon as it sees the shoe, then one 0.4 s burst every few seconds while it still does.
  • Sensor type is still open. A barrel type with an adjustable range, like the E18-D80NK, set to about 12 cm, is the safe pick for black leather. Across the belt at this spot there's only the casing box, about 270 mm away.

How much polish

ShoeSeenBursts0.5 mm0.8 mm

Per side, for the burst spacing chosen in the view. The belt polishes at 20 mm/s. Flow is an orifice estimate at about 4 bar (roughly 2.2 mL/s for 0.5 mm, 5.7 mL/s for 0.8 mm). Measure your own with water and a measuring cup.

Where the overspray goes

  • Mist that misses the shoe crosses toward the other post and lands on the belt and board.
  • The reflector starts 29 mm after the nozzles, so the upstream end of its white insert collects a little. The side ToF sensor is 149 mm away.
